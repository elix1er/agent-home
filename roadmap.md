# Roadmap — brainstorming

This is direction, not a specification.

The home could evolve from a few identity and goal files into an agent with a real role, recurring responsibilities, memory of completed work, reusable skills, and a reliable rhythm. Over time it may feel less like a configured tool and more like a coworker or employee with a clear purpose and history.

The next step is not more architecture. It is finding the first useful job.
